This mechanism relies on **Piezoelectric Energy Harvesting coupled to Solid-State Thermoelectric (Peltier) Modules**.

-   **The Physics:** As the 12.5-meter Vortex Resonance Cylinder vibrates under massive acoustic sound loads, the metal skin experiences severe bending forces and cyclic strain. By layering **Lead Zirconate Titanate (PZT)** crystalline arrays directly inside the form-molded chassis wall, this mechanical bending force is instantly converted into high-voltage electrical current.
-   **The Cooling Action:** This harvested current is routed straight into integrated internal micro-Peltier arrays embedded within the exhaust liner. The Peltier elements create an instantaneous temperature differential---forcing heat away from the inner exhaust tunnel walls and dumping it into outer containment heat sinks.
-   **The Benefit:** **The louder the engine screams, the more power it generates to cool itself.** It provides passive, solid-state cooling directly at the hottest friction barriers of your exhaust nozzle without spinning pumps or liquid coolant lines.
